# Bank Customer Churn Prediction and Risk Scoring

## Project Overview
This project uses machine learning to predict which bank customers may leave the bank. It helps identify customers who may need extra attention through retention campaigns.

## Objectives
- Analyse bank customer data.
- Identify factors related to customer churn.
- Train and compare machine learning models.
- Estimate the probability of customer churn.
- Classify customers into Low, Medium, and High risk categories.

## Tools and Technologies
- Python
- Google Colab
- Pandas and NumPy
- Matplotlib and Seaborn
- Scikit-learn
- Excel

## Methodology
1. Data collection and extraction
2. Data cleaning and preprocessing
3. Exploratory data analysis
4. Model training using Logistic Regression and Random Forest
5. Model evaluation
6. Churn probability prediction and risk classification

## Risk Categories
- Low Risk: Probability below 30%
- Medium Risk: Probability from 30% to below 60%
- High Risk: Probability of 60% or above

## Expected Outcome
The project aims to help banks identify customers at risk of leaving and plan suitable customer retention strategies.
